Diffwatch remembers a page and tells you what changed the next time you look. You give it an address, it saves a copy of the text, and on your next visit it shows the added and removed lines side by side.

I built it to keep an eye on a few documentation pages that change without announcement. Checking by hand meant reading the whole page again to find one new sentence.

It only compares text, so layout changes and images are ignored. That keeps the output short and easy to read. Some pages can’t be fetched from the browser, and for those it simply says so.
