Palette Pull takes a screenshot and gives back the handful of colours that make it up. I wrote it while working out the colours for a design and wanting to borrow the feel of a page I liked without eyeballing it.

It reads the image, groups pixels that are close in colour, and reports the largest groups as hex codes. The grouping is deliberately simple, so the results are good enough to start from but not perfect. Near-white backgrounds sometimes crowd out an accent colour, and I decided that was acceptable.

I consider it finished. It does the job in a few seconds, and I still use it whenever I start a new design.
