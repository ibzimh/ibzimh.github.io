Urdu Reader is a small web app for reading Urdu text when your vocabulary is still catching up. You paste in a paragraph, and each word gets a short English meaning underneath it, so you can keep reading instead of stopping to look things up.

It started as a way to get through the Urdu I was trying to read on my own. The word list is kept simple on purpose: one meaning per word, no grammar tables. If a word is missing, it says so and moves on.
