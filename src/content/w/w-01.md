For a long time I kept my notes in places only I could see. Half of them lived in a text editor, a few in the notes app on my phone, and the rest in my head, which is the worst place to keep anything. When I went looking for something I had worked out a year earlier, I usually had to work it out again.

This site is my attempt to fix that. It is a notebook that happens to be open to anyone. I write things down here when I have figured them out, and I try to write them so that I will understand them later, which also tends to make them readable by someone else.

I don’t have a schedule, and I don’t expect many people to read it. Some pieces will be long and some will be a few lines. If a note turns out to be useful to one other person, that is a good outcome, but it is not why I’m doing it.
